Skyrim Core Gameplay Elements

1. Exploration
   - Open-world exploration with diverse biomes: mountains, forests, caves, and dungeons.
   - Discovering locations: towns, cities, landmarks, and secret areas.
   - Fast travel system and map usage.

2. Combat
   - Melee combat with swords, axes, and other weapons.
   - Ranged combat with bows and crossbows.
   - Magic combat with various spells (destruction, conjuration, restoration).
   - Stealth mechanics, including sneak attacks and critical hits.
   - Block and parry system.

3. Character Development
   - Character customization at the beginning (race, gender, appearance).
   - Leveling system with experience points (XP) and leveling up.
   - Skill trees for combat, magic, stealth, and crafting abilities.
   - Perks that enhance skills and unlock special abilities.

4. Quests
   - Main story quests involving the Dragonborn and the fight against dragons.
   - Faction quests (Thieves Guild, Dark Brotherhood, Companions, etc.).
   - Side quests ranging from simple tasks to complex storylines.
   - Radiant quests (procedurally generated missions).

5. Crafting and Economy
   - Crafting systems: Smithing (weapons/armor), Alchemy (potions), Enchanting (magic items).
   - Collecting resources: mining, gathering plants, looting enemies.
   - Buying, selling, and trading with NPCs.

6. NPC Interaction
   - Dialogue system with NPCs, including choices that affect outcomes.
   - Companions and followers who can assist in combat and carry items.
   - Relationships: marriage, friendship, and rivalries.

7. World Events
   - Random encounters: bandit attacks, dragon fights, wildlife interactions.
   - Dynamic world changes based on player actions (e.g., civil war outcomes).

8. Magic and Shouts
   - Magic schools: Destruction, Restoration, Alteration, Conjuration, Illusion.
   - Dragon shouts (Thu'um) that grant powerful abilities.
   - Learning new shouts from dragon words scattered across the world.

9. Stealth and Thievery
   - Sneaking mechanics: staying hidden, pickpocketing, lockpicking.
   - Avoiding detection by staying in shadows and reducing noise.
   - Crimes and bounty system: stealing, assault, murder, and consequences.

10. Survival Elements
   - Managing health, stamina, and magicka.
   - Dealing with environmental hazards: cold, traps, poison.
   - Food and potion consumption to maintain stats.

11. Customization and Mods
   - Modifiable gameplay through community mods (adding new quests, items, mechanics).
   - In-game customization of items, homes, and gear.

12. Environmental Interaction
   - Interacting with objects: levers, doors, traps.
   - Climbing, swimming, and traversing varied terrain.
